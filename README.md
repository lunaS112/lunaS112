# Luna Sang

PhD student in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University and a member of the [Multi-Agent Robotic Systems Lab (MARS Lab)](https://sfumars.com/).

I work on control theory and Hamilton–Jacobi reachability, with a focus on learning-based and other computational methods for solving HJ problems.

## Research interests

- Control theory and Hamilton–Jacobi reachability
- Learning-based methods and HJ solvers
- Numerical methods and robotics
